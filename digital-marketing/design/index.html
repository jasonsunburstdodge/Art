Questions About Design

What's included in SilverXis design services?

Website Design & Development, Brand Identity, and Social & Campaign Design, working together as one consistent visual identity rather than three disconnected deliverables.

Do you work with businesses that already have a brand, or only new ones?

Both. Some clients need a brand identity built from scratch; others already have one that just needs to be applied consistently, or sharpened where it's grown inconsistent across their website, social, and campaigns.

Does design work include SEO and conversion, or just visuals?

Both. Search visibility, UX, messaging, and conversion are built into the design from the start, not layered on afterward, so the result looks credible and actually performs.

Should I redesign my website or rebuild it from scratch?

It depends on whether the underlying structure still serves you. We assess your current site's technical foundation, content, and UX before recommending a redesign or a full rebuild, rather than defaulting to the bigger, more expensive option.

How long does a design project take?

Timelines depend on scope, a brand identity refresh moves faster than a full website rebuild with new brand identity included, but most projects move in weeks, not months.
