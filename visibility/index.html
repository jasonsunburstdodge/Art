Questions About Modern Search

What's the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?

SEO helps search engines rank your site in results. AEO helps you get chosen as the direct answer, in a featured snippet, AI Overview, or voice response. GEO helps you get named and recommended inside AI chat platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. All three rely on the same foundation, but they target different moments in how people search.

Do I need to do all three?

Not as three separate budgets, but ignoring any one of them leaves a real gap. A well-optimized site with strong AEO but no GEO work can still be invisible in ChatGPT conversations, and strong GEO without an SEO foundation has nothing credible to point to.

Will AI search replace Google?

No, but it's changing what a first-page ranking is worth. Google now embeds AI Overviews directly into results, and a growing share of research starts on ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity instead. Businesses need to be visible across both systems, not choose one.

How long does it take to see results?

Technical SEO fixes and Google Business Profile improvements can show measurable movement within weeks. Competitive rankings, AI Overview appearances, and consistent GEO citations typically build over two to six months, since they depend on authority and trust signals that accumulate over time.

Can a small local business really compete for AI visibility?

Yes. AI platforms and local search reward clarity and relevance, not just size. A well-optimized local business with a complete Google Business Profile, consistent citations, and clear, specific content routinely outranks larger competitors who haven't done that work.
