What Is an IT Role Assessment?

"Before you spend weeks looking for the right person, make sure you're looking for the right person."

An IT role assessment takes a rough job description, or a plain-language explanation of who you need to hire, and turns it into a specific set of must-have and preferred requirements. It's built for whoever owns the hiring decision, not a recruiter's shorthand; you won't be asked for years-of-experience formulas or a wishlist of every skill that would be nice to have.

SilverXis uses your answers to suggest a seniority level, an engagement model, Contract Staffing, Contract-to-Hire, Direct Placement, or a Dedicated Team, and a starting list of qualifying questions to ask candidates, based on the requirements you actually flagged as non-negotiable.

You'll receive a personalized Role Clarity Assessment before you're ever asked for contact information. It's informational, meant to help you define the role clearly before recruiting starts, and it isn't a substitute for working directly with a SilverXis staffing specialist.

What is the Free IT Role Assessment?

It's a short, guided tool that turns a rough job description, or a plain description of who you need to hire, into a clear set of must-have and preferred requirements. It's built to help you define the role clearly before candidates ever get involved.

Do I need an existing job description to use it?

No. You can paste an existing job description and we'll pull out a starting set of requirements, or you can describe the role in your own words and we'll build the checklist from that instead.

What do I receive?

A Role Clarity Assessment showing your must-have and preferred requirements, a recommended candidate profile covering seniority, engagement model, and delivery model, suggested questions to ask candidates, and a downloadable summary you can share with your team.

Is this a substitute for working with a recruiter?

No. It's meant to sharpen the role before recruiting starts, so a SilverXis recruiter can qualify candidates against real requirements instead of a vague title. Any recommendation should still be confirmed with a SilverXis staffing specialist.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere unless you choose to submit your contact information at the end to have a SilverXis recruiter review your results.
