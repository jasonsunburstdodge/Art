Questions About Building Software Around Your Business

When does a business need custom software instead of off-the-shelf software?

Off-the-shelf software works well when your needs closely match what the product was designed to do. Custom software development becomes valuable when your workflows, integrations, data, customer experience, or growth requirements can't be addressed without significant workarounds or compromises. The goal isn't to build custom software simply because you can. It's to build it when doing so creates meaningful business value.

How much does custom software development cost?

The cost of custom software depends on what you're building, the complexity of the application, required integrations, infrastructure, security requirements, and the amount of development involved. We start with discovery so the requirements, priorities, technical approach, and expected business value are understood before determining the appropriate scope.

Can custom software integrate with the systems we already use?

Yes. Through APIs, systems integration, and data integration, custom applications can often connect with existing business platforms, databases, cloud services, and legacy systems. That allows you to add new capabilities without automatically replacing technology that's already working.

Can you modernize legacy software without replacing everything?

Often, yes. Legacy modernization doesn't necessarily mean rebuilding an entire system. Depending on the existing technology, modernization can involve updating individual applications, moving workloads to the cloud, improving interfaces, connecting systems through APIs, modernizing databases, or gradually replacing outdated components while keeping critical operations running.

How can custom software prepare a business for AI and automation?

AI and automation depend on more than adding an AI tool. Your applications, systems, workflows, and data need to be able to support them. Building connected systems, accessible data, scalable infrastructure, and well-defined workflows creates a stronger foundation for introducing AI, intelligent automation, analytics, and other emerging capabilities as they become valuable to the business.
